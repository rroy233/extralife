# Code of Conduct

ExtraLife is a community project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to help keep them welcoming and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information, credentials, raw Codex transcripts, or private project data.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized attention, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. This applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rroy233). Include a link or a short description and any relevant context. Do not include private transcripts or credentials.

The maintainer will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and take action proportionate to the situation. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, or restricting participation in project spaces.
